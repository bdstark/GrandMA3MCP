# Code of conduct

GrandMA3MCP welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. Everyone participating in this
project is expected to help keep its community respectful, inclusive and safe.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and patient. Explain assumptions and welcome questions from newcomers.
- Discuss ideas, evidence and code constructively. Disagreement is welcome; personal attacks are not.
- Respect people's identities, boundaries, privacy and time.
- Accept feedback, acknowledge mistakes and help repair harm.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, intimidation, sexual harassment, demeaning remarks, deliberate
personal attacks and publishing private information without consent are unacceptable. This includes
unwelcome sexual content, repeated unwanted contact and retaliation against someone who raises a
concern or participates in a review.

## Scope

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project-managed
spaces, as well as communications made while officially representing the project. Conduct elsewhere
may be considered when it directly affects participants' safety or ability to take part here.

## Report a concern

Contact the maintainer privately at **[bdstark@gmail.com](mailto:bdstark@gmail.com)** with the subject
`GrandMA3MCP conduct report`. Include what happened, relevant links or dates, and any immediate concerns
or preferred follow-up. Share only information needed to understand the incident. You do not need to
confront the person involved or post a public report.

Reports will be handled as privately as practical. Details will be shared only as needed to assess the
report, respond to it or meet legal obligations; absolute confidentiality cannot be promised. Reports
and moderation are handled on a best-effort basis, without a guaranteed response time.

This project currently has a single maintainer and no independent appeals panel. If a report concerns
the maintainer, or you do not feel comfortable using this contact, you may use GitHub's own abuse
reporting tools for conduct on GitHub. Those reports are handled by GitHub, not this project.

For software vulnerabilities, use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit or remove content, close or lock conversations, request a change in behavior,
issue a warning, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation. The response should reflect the
severity, impact and repetition of the conduct; serious incidents may warrant an immediate restriction.
The same standards apply to maintainers and contributors.

Where practical, the person affected by a moderation decision will be told the reason and scope without
revealing private reporter information. They may request reconsideration through the same private
contact, providing relevant context. Good-faith reports and requests for reconsideration must not lead
to retaliation.
